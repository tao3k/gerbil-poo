#+title: Cold feedback publication costs

The 10,000-group study runs the actual POO publication stages. Building
group values dominated the two staged samples at 1.59 and 1.78 seconds CPU.
SCC discovery and certification took 15.86 and 15.40 ms CPU. Each sample
constructs all group values and runs final approval.
