#+title: Fresh-process paired benchmark protocol

* Reproduction

Run =just benchmark-metadata-paired= through the existing package and native
Scheme executables. The current recipe owns preparation and the aggregate
bound. Its child protocol retains its own deadline and heap fence. Outputs
stay under ignored =.build/metadata/=; CI publishes logs/artifacts rather than
committing run data. No additional benchmark runner is required.

The authoritative dimensions and side order are in
[[file:../../benchmarks/metadata-paired-cases.ss][metadata-paired-cases.ss]]; the native supervisor is
[[file:../../benchmarks/metadata-paired-suite.ss][metadata-paired-suite.ss]]. Consume those declarations instead of duplicating
scenario counts or strategy inventories in documentation.

* Comparison and admission

Each pair uses fresh child processes with matching source, binary, toolchain,
compiler options, workload and prefix history. Preserve declared side order,
rotation, repetitions and any original warmup protocol. Do not add prewarming
to a frozen acceptance workload or mix forced-GC and natural regimes.

The native checker requires complete ordered coverage, finite measurements,
current source/binary identities and the final completion record. Missing,
duplicate, stale or reordered rows fail admission. Source identity checks bind
observations to the run; old measurements do not qualify a changed implementation.

Report CPU including GC, GC cost, admitted allocation and process exit under
their actual definitions. Allocation crossing collection or a negative native
counter delta is unavailable. Keep every paired direction and failed control;
a favourable pooled median cannot replace mixed or adverse paired outcomes.

Separate first construction, selection, binding, publication and sustained use.
Their scopes may overlap; an isolated selection win does not establish a full
call or first-EVAL improvement. Optional strategy selection retains its source
ownership and effect-boundary contracts independently of measured performance.

* Versioned result policy

Use a concise matrix alongside the implementation when a result is needed for
review. Name the measured operation, reference/candidate revisions, workload,
units and acceptance limits. Raw Scheme/JSON rows, child stdout, resource
receipts, repeated platform runs and abandoned variants remain local or in CI
artifacts. Historical per-run narratives are available in Git history.

For the shared output policy see [[file:results.org][results.org]].
