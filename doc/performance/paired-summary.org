The historical recorded study contains 192 fresh native child processes: four scenarios,
natural/collected regimes, four rounds and three adjacent comparison pairs.
Each process constructs all controls in the same order and executes four fixed
warmup passes. Side order is ABBA and pair positions rotate. Natural does not
force a collection; collected explicitly collects immediately before sampling.
Children are capped at 20 seconds and 1 GiB; compilation and the aggregate
study each have a separate 90-second bound. No local test/build ran during timing.

Controls are independent preparation, uncaptured selection (the previous
per-call generic lookup, retained only in the benchmark), captured dynamic
selection and a reusable immutable preparation cache. Selector-only samples use
4096 repetitions; family, joint publication and 15-node workflow samples use 128.
Semantic checks include descriptor/binding reuse, current-snapshot invocation,
atomic budget refusal, shared dependencies and selective rebuilding.
