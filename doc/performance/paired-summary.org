#+TITLE: Fresh-process preparation selector controls

* Scope and reproducibility
This is local native Gerbil/Gambit evidence for generalized prototype protocol
future work. Reproduce with =GERBIL_PATH="$PWD/.build/env" just benchmark-metadata-paired=
after installing this package in a suitable isolated Gerbil environment. No Lean
checkout, Python timing driver, checked-in raw matrix or compressed archive is
required. Outputs stay under ignored =.build/metadata/=. The driver validates
current source and binary digests, complete coverage and metric admission.

The current study contains 192 fresh native child processes: four scenarios,
natural/collected regimes, four rounds and three adjacent comparison pairs.
Each process constructs all controls in the same order and executes four fixed
warmup passes. Side order is ABBA and pair positions rotate. Natural does not
force a collection; collected explicitly collects immediately before sampling.
Children are capped at 20 seconds and 1 GiB; compilation and the aggregate
study each have a separate 90-second bound. No local test/build ran during timing.

Controls are independent preparation, uncaptured selection (the previous
per-call generic lookup, retained only in the benchmark), captured dynamic
selection and a reusable immutable preparation plan. Selector-only samples use
4096 repetitions; family, joint publication and 15-node workflow samples use 128.
Semantic checks include descriptor/binding reuse, current-snapshot invocation,
atomic budget refusal, shared dependencies and selective rebuilding.

* Diagnosis
The historical [[file:selective-summary.org][single-process result]] had natural CPU medians of 255.750
versus 222.422 microseconds (about 15%). Incoming GC history was uncontrolled;
its per-round selector/independent collection counts were 1/0, 21/0, 31/31 and
24/32. That comparison cannot isolate selector dispatch overhead.

The fresh-process natural joint comparison independent -> uncaptured has a
median paired CPU difference of -0.172 microseconds; the old 15% regression is
not reproduced. Prefix collection counts match in all 96 pairs, while the full
three-field prefix counters differ in 17 pairs. Equal collection counts do not
establish identical heap contents or allocation history. The measurements do
not prove that GC alone caused the historical difference.

Selector-only natural marginal CPU medians are approximately 0.301 us for the
uncaptured function, 0.252 us with its immutable callback captured using
=:std/func compose1=, and 0.064 us with the plan callback. Capturing removes
about 0.053 us per selector call in this local paired comparison. This does not
imply an equivalent whole-publication speedup.

The plan remains optional. Joint publication still regresses versus captured
dynamic selection in both regimes despite the selector microbenchmark saving;
the larger workflow improves locally. The existing always-shared example
source remains its default. Choose a plan only under stable immutable prices
and measured workload/support; changed arity takes the ordinary selection path.
Changed costs require a new plan. Existing source/support/approval checks apply.

Lean proves selection equivalence, hit/fallback counts and conditional
amortization including cold setup, first selection and every support guard.
It also proves per-pair CPU/GC decomposition and cancellation of an additive
ABBA position term. It does not prove a concrete compiler/collector cost model,
identical heaps, arbitrary drift cancellation or an end-to-end speedup.
