* Construction and proof boundary

The reviewed Racket/Appendix B construction separates ordered labelled fragments
from their effective method. The companion book's method-cache exercise requests
an eight-entry effective-method LRU. This addition advances generalized-protocol
future work; forwarding and Simula APIs remain explicit extensions of that basis.

=multimethod.prepared-*= specializes standard, simple, forwarding, inner and Simula
combinations through the existing typed sparse dispatcher. One eight-entry native
resolution LRU stores candidates and unconditional effective methods together.
Guarded shapes reselect/recompile each call; register/contribute/guard/erase empty
the cache. Current arguments, forwarded payload validation, missing-method refusal
and prefix failure remain observable. Results are never cached.

=multimethod.nested= gives a first-class generic an explicit child owner. A method
returns result/child-successor; the ordinary certificate/family/version chain
transports both caches. A child may itself own a child. Child method-index AND
invoker callback identities must match; edited children require checked publication.
The nested calling convention is explicit and distinct from standard side methods.
Two reusable native invoker descriptors specialize to compact private records;
cache successors do not clone lazy POO descriptors or hide mutable body caches.

Lean proves effective/candidate cache soundness from fresh creation through actual
calls and register/contribute/registerWhen edits, per-call guarded fallback,
current-definition agreement, prepared combination equality, nested success and
failure, and optional concrete child-provenance admission. Native pointer identity
is an implementation premise, not equality of arbitrary Lean functions. Closed
Lean request examples are executable checks in addition to the generic proofs.

=EffectiveCosts= reuses =TemplateCosts= and charges cold sparse resolution,
unguarded misses, every guarded compilation, allocation, per-call transport and
supplied excess-GC prices. Common construction/binding/approval remains in the
base only when genuinely common. =chooseWorkflow= separately charges different
fresh/prepared carrier-construction, binding and admission bases; a cold pipeline
can save carrier work even when caching alone cannot amortize. Cold and
configuration-fee laws prevent an unpaid setup claim. These are
conditional operation budgets, not native compiler or GC predictions.
