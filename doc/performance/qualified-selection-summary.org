* Configuration and fallback

=SelectionCacheCosts.= charges setup, actual changed-input preparations, support
fallbacks, support guards and an explicit extra-GC budget. Unchanged contexts
never enter selection. Ties choose dynamic. Prices/horizons are supplied premises,
not measured constants embedded in the implementation.

=choose-source= compiles the fixed policy once at configuration. Dynamic returns
the exact source, without forcing support or allocating a cache. Cached composes
the existing immutable cache mixin and retains its changed-arity fallback. Source
or price changes require explicit reconfiguration. Tests count one real budget
decision across configuration, fresh source construction and retained revisions.

The =configure= scenario separately charges fresh price-object validation and
choice, source construction and input-callback preparation. =cold-family= measures
repeated fresh sources AFTER fixed policy configuration. =cold-checks= charges a
fresh parent's first invariant compilation and binding, not a free warm template.
Lean =configuration_covered= requires the one-time cost be covered by selector
saving; dynamic fallback alone does not make configuration free.

The joint natural comparison shows both a GC and residual component, while the
collected comparison still regresses slightly. Qualified standard/simple results
remain mixed. This is evidence for explicit strategy choice, not a universal
speedup or proof that every difference was caused by GC.

Caching after shared expansion/checks itself is still unstable: shared-checks ->
shared-checks-cached has joint CPU delta +3.297 natural / -3.363 collected and
workflow -0.395 / +6.098. A selector-only timing cannot select these complete
workflows safely. Configured fallback removes repeated policy interpretation by
source identity; its one-time configuration remains visibly expensive.

Invariant-check sharing is also opt-in. expanded -> shared-checks has cold-checks
CPU delta +3.598 natural / +1.742 collected, with 0/4 faster pairs in BOTH regimes.
The first compilation/stamp/descriptor cost is real. Joint delta -1.496 / +2.605
and workflow +4.133 / -4.871 remain mixed. Fresh binding alone differs by about
0.008us natural, so it does not establish a meaningful complete-workflow gain.
The ordinary fresh-check construction remains available.

There were 21 prefix allocation-vector mismatches among 960 process pairs, across
12 of the 240 scenario/regime/comparison aggregates; prefix GC counts matched in
all pairs. Even matching counters do not imply identical heaps. The key
configure/cold-family/joint/workflow fallback rows above have zero prefix-vector
mismatches. Simple qualified comparisons have allocation-history mismatches and
must not be treated as isolated selector/GC causal evidence. Collections make
allocation deltas unavailable in full publications; no allocation reduction is
claimed from those raw counters.
