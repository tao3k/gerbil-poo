* Native matched study
144 independent bounded children: three scenarios, natural/collected GC, direct
pair and two A/A pairs, four balanced ABBA rounds. Every child warms all four
actions in the same order, validates the actual price 33, and admits current
source/binary identities and complete EOF. No build/test ran during measurement.

Cold means a fresh snapshot and two-stage observation of an ALREADY EXISTING,
C3-cached diamond. Module/graph creation and initial C3 cold linearization are
outside this study. Both cold sides intentionally execute the same function.
Observation compares a reused compiled snapshot with fresh snapshot preparation
before each finite observation. Hot compares a retained cooked callable with
repeated cooking/observation before a price call. Reused setup is outside these
phase timings; cold is reported separately, not silently amortized away. These
are phase controls, not an end-to-end service throughput claim.

Delta is hoisted minus fresh, median of within-pair microseconds. N/C denotes
natural/collected. Residual subtracts recorded GC CPU, not inferred GC causes.
