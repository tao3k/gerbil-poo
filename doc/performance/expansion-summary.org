#+TITLE: Shared generic expansion and publication-stage controls

* Construction and scope
This advances generalized prototype/MOP future work. The opt-in
~generic-family.methods.instantiate<-shared-expansion~ reconstructs one closed,
immutable policy/cooked environment for a changed generic's seed and methods.
It removes their duplicate expansion and two adapter closures. The extra
registration-provenance callback remains charged separately. The default
~Projection.~ keeps separate reconstruction for constructors that observe fresh
identity or mutate reconstructed values. No new module namespace is introduced.

~examples/shared-contracts.expanded-model / expanded-workflow-model~ compose this
method with captured dynamic contract selection, existing constructors/wrappers,
cooked interfaces, negative watches and joint approval. Actual call-count controls
show cold reconstruction 6 -> 3 for three generics; invoice-only revision expands
once, retains reserve's warm certificate and invokes against the current invoice.
Registration and negative failures retain their service/phase, while warm-budget
refusal retains the exact old publication. All 27 native test modules passed.

Lean ~GenericFamily.SharedExpansion.shared_eq / patch_eq / negative_eq~ preserve
constructor inputs, registration results and the negative decision on the same
patched generic. Existing empty-seed/source/cache proofs remain required.
~PreparationCosts.ExpansionCosts~ charges reconstruction, adapters and the extra
guard; the publication bound requires the guard to fit the saving. The collection
bound additionally requires an explicit budget for GC excess. No compiler,
collector, mutable-pointer semantics or unrestricted Scheme equivalence is proved.

* Fair native protocol
Run ~just benchmark-metadata-paired~ in the package's normal Gerbil environment.
This needs no Lean checkout or Python timing driver. There are 640 admitted fresh
native children / 320 adjacent pairs: ten scenarios, natural/collected regimes,
four ABBA rounds and four rotated comparison pairs among five controls.

The controls are independent, uncaptured, captured dynamic, selection cache and
shared generic expansion. The last uses exactly the dynamic contract source;
selector/input/contract-only comparisons between those sides are A/A controls.
Each child constructs and warms ALL controls in fixed order before measuring ONE
side. Collected explicitly runs GC before sampling; natural does not reset it.
Selector batches use 4096 repetitions, other stages 128. Child limit is 20s/1GiB,
compile and aggregate limits are separately 90s. No local builds/tests overlapped
timing. All records, binaries and full diagnostics remain ignored under .build.

The driver admits complete source/binary-bound receipts only and reports pair
ranges, direction, GC excess, allocation availability and prefix counters. Tests
reject missing/duplicate/reordered/wrong-side/stale/incomplete/nonfinite data and
incorrect allocation admission. Full prefix counter vectors and GC counts match
in all 320 pairs; this still does not establish identical heaps or remove arbitrary
temporal drift. Four pairs per row are finite local evidence, not significance.

Default captured-dynamic side from the directly matched shared-expansion pair;
all times are microseconds per operation. CPU-GC is calculated within each sample
before taking its median. Independent median columns must NOT be added. These
callbacks have different semantic oracles, so isolated times must NOT be summed
as a decomposition of full publication. Collections are per 128-operation batch.

The bind-only natural/collected difference is largely GC CPU in these batches;
its residual remains about 0.4us. Executor-only rebinding avoids descriptor
construction but still pays family guards/tables/traces and wrapper binding.
The generic-construction stage is much larger than the selector stage here,
motivating shared reconstruction rather than assuming selector micro-savings
explain the full pipeline.

Raw signed allocation counters are preserved. Admitted no-GC input batches report
256.25 bytes/op, collected bind batches 512.25 bytes/op; these include measurement
counter overhead. Full generic/joint batches collect and allocation is unavailable.
Fewer reconstruction/adapter calls are established by instrumentation/modeling;
exact full-publication allocated bytes or a GC bound are not inferred from them.

* Shared expansion: direct paired comparison
Negative delta means shared expansion is faster. Each row has four direct pairs;
range is the minimum/maximum actual per-pair CPU delta. CPU/GC/residual columns
are independent medians and need not add. Constructor/wrapper/admission semantics
and dynamic contract selection are held fixed; this compares current strategies,
not a historical-binary before/after claim.

Both observed GC CPU and residual CPU improve; it is not a selector-only saving.
This does not establish how every allocation or collector cycle caused each
microsecond. Keep sharing opt-in under the pure/immutable constructor contract.

* Variability and remaining adverse results
Selection-cache-only savings still do not predict end-to-end gains. Actual paired
CPU deltas dynamic -> cached, including the negative samples:

| Operation | Regime | Median delta | Actual range | Faster pairs |
|-----------+--------+--------------+--------------+--------------|
| joint | natural | -5.242 | -12.328 .. +4.070 | 3/4 |
| joint | collected | +0.801 | -13.320 .. +5.266 | 2/4 |
| workflow | natural | +4.953 | -8.922 .. +9.578 | 1/4 |
| workflow | collected | +3.883 | -8.648 .. +13.344 | 2/4 |

Captured selection itself saves about 0.059us natural / 0.050us collected, and
selection-cache lookup another 0.183/0.185us in selector-only controls. The shared
expansion selector A/A pair spans -0.005..+0.005us natural. Identical constructor
pairs span roughly -0.98..+3.58us natural; identical approval pairs
-1.55..+0.09us. These are observed noise references, not calibrated confidence
intervals or permission to discard bad results. Previous regressions remain in
[[file:paired-summary.org][historical paired controls]]. Selection caching remains optional.
