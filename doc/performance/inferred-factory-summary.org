#+title: Inferred factory construction and checked publication

* Scope and construction
This advances the paper's type-protocol future work. The Racket appendix supplies
Self/Super constraints and explicit prototype specialization, rather than this
finite inference algorithm. Gerbil retains its existing Type compiler, positional
specialization, declared generics, FUN refinement and IO publication.

Only a private, completely searched unique solution can bypass repeated pure
argument/bound checks and a second parse/copy of its private schema. Raw substituted syntax is retained separately from the
normalized public alternative, including controlled Mu. Instance syntax and
substitution are copied independently from mutable public search reports. Live
prototype parameters and direct bound descriptor revisions are still refreshed
before ordinary descriptor, receiver and method declaration construction.
Composite descriptors remain fresh; intrinsic leaves keep standard library identity.
Explicit instantiation still runs the full checker. Exhaustion, ambiguity and
unsupported syntax remain refusals; removing visits may admit work formerly over
quota, so this is not equality of exact quota thresholds.

bind-evidence retains with-meaning and its customization, fresh binding identity,
Boolean admission, parent-first short circuit and exact subject/descriptor/context
checks. One Formula fuses the known atom and conjunction. The service workflow
also shares its private invariant invocation template; every publication still
validates and binds current values. Neither change caches approval or results.

* Proof boundary
Lean CheckedAssignment / checkedUnique connects the completed finite solver to
each original constraint. instantiatePrepared_eq preserves the same live refresh
and assembler, including errors; instantiatePrepared_refuses retains stale-source
refusal. Formula.conjoinAtom_holds and the false/error/true laws retain logical
meaning and decision order. accepted_comparison_work counts eliminated abstract
replays only. Copying, compiler correctness, total allocation, GC and wall time
are not proved. Runtime custom predicates, projection purity and source metadata
stability keep their existing explicit premises.

and collected heaps, seven phases, six AB/BA pairs and three A/A controls: 504

100 measured operations. Phases are factory, action construction, prepared call,

of allocator history. Module initialization stays outside phase timings and must
be charged separately for whole-process startup claims.

* Guarded declaration catalog reuse
The next increment starts at cabc40f29ee4719ce72562a59afc3e1473397032.
declare-instance previously reflected the entire effective Slot directory for
typed forcing and reflected it again for root Function/CaseFunction admission.
It now passes that same local directory to final admission. No process cache or
new public certificate is introduced. Source/binding comparison, current Slot and
method checks, exact root coverage, callback order and failure precedence remain.
Nested Object catalogs keep their original traversal.

The existing active construction frame guards the receiver from first reflection
through final admission. Supported writes to its registered prototype/Slot/method
metadata dependencies check the entire affected closure before invalidation and
refuse when they reach this receiver. This establishes local directory stability
under serialized supported edits. Unregistered host mutation and concurrent
mutation are outside the existing protocol. Neither catalog contents nor old
approval results are reused across factories.

Gerbil's standard reflection and list utilities still construct the catalog; the
factory chain removes a repeated projection rather than implementing another
reflection engine. Hidden rich methods, lazy ordinary fields, deferred metadata
edits, exact refusal and successful fresh retry have regression controls.
Lean admitReflected_eq / admitReflected_refuses preserve complete forcing and
admission results under explicit catalog stability, including changed cache state.
reflected_catalog_work counts one removed projection; allocation and timing still
need the same fair paired measurements. This remains bounded future work.

The first reflected-directory-only study did not establish a stable speedup.
