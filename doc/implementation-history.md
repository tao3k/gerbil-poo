### Executable alignment status

The standard qualified combiner and simple policies now have native POO
constructors in `method-combination.ss`, integrated through `multimethod.standard`
and `multimethod.simple` with existing Type/guard/negative-watch publication.
`examples/service-combination.ss` composes them with shared contract checks and
atomic multi-service revisions. This implements a concrete part of the above
method-combination direction; the unified generalized MOP remains future work.
See `alignment.md` for the Racket/Lean/Scheme correspondence and the remaining
general MOP, dependent catalog/wire and lens/migration boundaries. Prepared effective methods and explicit
nested cache successor transport are now implemented; their horizon-dependent
costs remain under native paired measurement. Forwarding,
inner and Simula now have native constructors and joint-service examples; their
control semantics and explicit invoice dependencies are mapped in alignment.md.


Declared unconditional shape preparation now addresses Simula assembly repeated
by discarded admission probes and first real calls. Child-only and both-level
models use the existing projection/certificate chain. Body/guard execution,
actual Type/C4 lookup, negative checking and resource admission remain explicit;
zero-budget cold publication may refuse preallocated entries. See alignment.md
and performance/simula-publication-summary.org for proof and native cost boundaries.

- Simula factory/call follow-up: compiled prefix/suffix callbacks with standard
  folds, fused fresh construction and immutable-template binding. A staged native
  Model combines captured initial calls with checked shared-definition handoff;
  first owner change still rebuilds all generics. See alignment.md and
  performance/simula-factory-summary.org for proofs, controls and remaining limits.

- Simula owner/call follow-up: opt-in symbol-sized family storage and bound current
  contexts, with complete owner/watch rechecking. The native paired matrix now
  separates post-handoff calls and first/subsequent revision allocation. See
  performance/simula-owner-summary.org; low-call revision-heavy workloads retain
  the shared-context alternative, with full binding/setup/GC cost accounting.

The owner-storage follow-up adds `methods.refresh<-empty-storage`: the existing
HashTable.new protocol supplies independent empty eq tables, avoiding variadic
constructor dispatch and copy/stage/prune preparation. Every current owner cell
is still constructed and checked. Binding is measured separately from first/later

### Bounded recursive request and memo implementation

`recursive.ss`, `recursive-cache.ss` and `recursive-handling.ss` now implement the
explicit request/FUN chain, exact program-owner memo, separate source errors and
cycle/exhaustion diagnostics, and repeated CURRENT Type admission. Native catalog

### Generalized hooks: finite native bridge implemented, future work continues

`generalized-prototype.ss` now composes checked native C3 local declarations,

### Focused local assembly and surrounding writes

`prepare-focused` and `prepare-focused-inheritance` now compose an entire local

the direct subcomponent construction item above: repeated outer `.cc` versions
and their slot/precedence-cache preparation are avoided. Existing C3, lenses,
method combinations and FUN observers are reused. Nested tenant/approval examples
cover independent audits and fresh configuration binding. Pure lens laws and
extensional observations are required; identity/ancestry inspection, arbitrary

The focused envelope also enforces its Bool approval domain at selection time;
0/string truthiness is refused rather than widening the Lean premise. Three
final-source native studies supersede admission of the initial source, retaining
all older observations as historical data.

### Reusable current-context observation chains

Implemented the finite fixed-source path: `bind-context-observer` captures one
prepared graph, selects current base and seed, and composes through native FUN
map with standard function composition. `bind-envelope-report` reuses that chain
across service/tenant approval revisions without reusing old admission or cooked
results. One-off observations avoid a temporary binder. Each declaration is
commented; Scheme remains standalone. `alignment.md` links Lean semantic laws,
supplied cost bounds, complex native controls and the paired benchmark summary.
Automatic mutable-source invalidation, arbitrary hook-law discovery and full
reflective MOP remain open; phase improvements are not whole-workflow speed laws.

## Coordinator exit and explicit recovery

`recursive-approvals.call-with-submissions` now accepts the existing coordinator
`on-exit:` outcome-thunk convention, after scope cleanup. A handler can open a fresh
scope and explicitly prepare against survivor progress; it cannot reopen the old
queue. Errors and multiple values retain their original meaning, and policy errors
propagate once. Eight actual SCC/provider/computed rounds alternate survivor and
recovered installers, with 256 attempts, repeated approval ABA, joint refusal,
historical request/reference rejection and current refine/bind-evidence retry.
Nine Lean lifecycle laws and matching finite graph examples cover this serialized
generalized MOP future work. See [recursive-recovery.md](recursive-recovery.md) for
the scope, host controls and remaining concurrency/interruption boundaries.
