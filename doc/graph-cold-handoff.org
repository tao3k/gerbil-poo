#+title: Cold Graph ownership handoff
#+language: en

* Contract and correspondence

The former =capture-partition= continuation calls =partition-captured= and
then reads two fields from its compact capture. This retains the complete
CSR payload across normalization and the independent certificate. Ownership
needs only the already admitted position index and direction bits.

[[file:graph-cold-handoff.lean][The standalone Lean contract]] proves
=handoff_exact= for arbitrary stateful discovery, certification and ownership
actions, including errors and their observable state. =projection_ignores_payload=
proves that the projected view has no payload field. Both proofs use no axioms.
Run =lean doc/graph-cold-handoff.lean= with the installed Lean 4.35.0.
This is a narrow projection refinement, not a new SCC completeness proof or
a theorem about compiler root liveness, physical memory or currentness.

[[file:graph-cold-handoff.sysml][The SysML contract]] maps PackedCapture to
=compact-graph=, OwnershipView to its positions/direction fields, and
ProjectOwnership to their early lexical binding. No new runtime view object
is allocated. The private completion continuation closes over these fields,
keys and symbolic rows. =partition-captured= and =finish-partition= pass this
continuation through their existing tail handoff. =own-partition= consumes
the fields directly. Non-owned and local-repair callers retain their default
single-result protocol. All public entrypoints remain unchanged.

The refinement assumes the admitted capture fields are immutable during the
pure discovery/certificate interval. Existing discovery writes private state
vectors, not capture positions, targets, offsets or direction. Author capture
remains before this interval. Certificate failure still prevents ownership.

* Native validation

=benchmarks/cold-handoff-test.ss= belongs to the explicit development build.
After ordinary compilation, use =just test-file= on its installed interface.
The existing Just/Gerbil runner owns heap limits and success markers.

Its bounded diagnostic keeps a weak reference to the actual compact capture,
forces GC at the post-certificate boundary and compares retained/projected
continuations. Weak references also cover names, offsets, targets and positions:
the projected case releases the first four and retains positions. This tests
the real private discovery/certificate handoff, not interception of the public
factory. A private-binding interception experiment was ineffective against
compiled internal calls and was removed; its failed log is not a negative
control. The bounded contract runs separately through the existing CI Just
entrypoint, before the unchanged full Graph studies. It adds no runner or job.
The cold paired study uses the same source, capture, discovery, certificate
and ownership implementation in ABBA order at 100K and 1M owners. The reference
keeps the former late field projection. Each arm must call every author and
retain the same complete certificate work. No GC is forced in timed samples;
cross-GC allocation deltas remain unqualified. Existing multi-shape/delta and
publication suites remain independent correctness requirements.

The post-change six-module Graph/recursive-publication integration passes all
176 cases, including all Module/Harness/final markers and natural exit zero.
The Lean contract and strict SysML check pass. Native array-lifetime checks
pass independently. A first benchmark fixture failed due to a POO same-name
=keys= binding and was corrected. Failed evidence stays in local artifacts.

* Exact-policy native observation

The final standalone source passes both cases, with Module/Harness/final OK
and natural exit zero. The retained reference includes the same finite-roster
admission as the former public entry. One ABBA block yields two samples per
arm and size; complete membership, order, owner and row output is validated
outside each timed cold-build window and charged separately.

| Owners | Retained CPU median s | Projected CPU median s |
|--------+-----------------------+------------------------|
| 100K   |              1.493301 |               1.539924 |
| 1M     |             18.767117 |              17.372073 |

At 1M, retained samples are 19.940815/17.593418 CPU seconds and 62/46 GCs;
projected samples are 17.512147/17.231999 seconds and 45/45 GCs. The final
retained sample is close to the projected samples. At 100K the direction
reverses. This does not establish a stable CPU improvement. All cold samples
cross GC, so allocated bytes remain unqualified; signed raw deltas stay in
local artifacts. The Just execution takes 89.70 s wall and 86.01 s CPU; the
outer resource window takes 90.51 s wall and 86.07 s CPU, reporting maximum
RSS 2065924096 bytes. This mixed-arm maximum
does not attribute RSS savings to either arm and cannot be compared to a
different multi-shape process.

The demonstrated gain is bounded retention: the private handoff can release
the capture, names, offsets and targets while retaining positions. This is
not a solution to the remaining 1M public cold-build cost or Core 10K EVAL.
Exact-head CI and the unchanged end-to-end gates remain open. No consumer
pin, release or full Core/Module acceptance follows from these checks.
