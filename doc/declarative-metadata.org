#+TITLE: DeclarativeMetadata and ServiceMetadata

* Scope

This advances the generalized-prototype future work about method, type and
logical metadata. It implements runtime checks; dependent proof receipts and
arbitrary callback effects remain outside Scheme's guarantees. There is no Lean
checkout dependency. Names refer to LeanPoo.Functional.DeclarativeMetadata and
LeanPoo.Functional.ServiceMetadata.

* One native expression protocol

The public model follows ~fun.ss~ and ~io.ss~: ~define-type~, prototype methods,
~.defgeneric~, and derived method mixins. Private records carry failures only.

- ~Declaration.~ exposes ~name~ and ~.compile~. ~install~ passes compiled edits
  directly to DispatchExtension; no second method-registration engine exists.
- ~Formula.~ exposes ~.holds~ and inherits ~.check~ from
  ~methods.check<-holds~. ~both~, ~either~, ~implies~, and ~deny~ compose decisions
  at construction. Calls retain short circuiting and Boolean atom checks.
- ~Predicate.~ inherits ~Type.~. Both refinements and ~typed~ descriptors use
  existing ~element?~ and ~validate~. There is no separate type interpreter.
- ~Contract.~ exposes ~.wrap~ and inherits ~.invoke~ from
  ~methods.invoke<-wrap~. A wrapper turns an existing call into another call;
  wrappers can nest. Additional source context is forwarded unchanged, while
  input and relational checks see only the argument row.

#+begin_src scheme
(def checked (metadata.wrap (service.reservation 10) execute))
(checked '(2)) ; two values: the checked business result and exact successor state
#+end_src

Construction of a contract and binding a source are separate costs. CyclicPublication
binds wrappers once while preparing the candidate. Warm calls reuse the wrapper;
cache-only successors share it. A revision reruns the retained contract builder
against the newly cooked view, preventing old capacity captures from leaking.

* Failure and state

Input refusal precedes execution. Raised source errors preserve their cause;
returned business errors remain values. Output refusal precedes logic refusal.
A refused output/rule returns no tentative cache successor. ~metadata.publish~
returns the identical old state on refusal; family publication retains the exact
old version when construction, metadata preparation, or admission refuses.

Callbacks, type descriptors, formula objects and published views must remain
stable and pure. POO metadata is open and extensible, not tamper-proof evidence.
External mutation and arbitrary effects cannot be undone by retaining a value.

* Service composition

~examples/service-metadata.ss~ reuses CyclicAccess's ten Boolean equations and
cooked view. It declares heterogeneous quote/reserve/cancel signatures, compiles
existing dispatch edits, attaches a positive-units/output refinement and the
permission/conservation rule, and probes the completed family before publication.
A changed capacity/price/invoice rebuilds both implementations and contracts.
Consent errors remain business results. Invalid capacity, excess quote price or
revoked access refuses the whole candidate.

This example adapts ServiceMetadata's service concepts to the existing finite
Scheme graph. It does not implement Lean's fifteen-node selective builder or
claim complete equivalence of those graph representations. Negative watches
continue to be provided by DispatchExtension and its existing diamond example.

* Evidence

~t/declarative-metadata-test.ss~ exercises native inheritance, Type interoperability,
wrapper composition, formula decisions, edit identity and failure priority.
~t/service-metadata-test.ss~ exercises independent cache warming, freshly bound
cooked contracts, business errors and atomic refusals.

~just benchmark-metadata~ compiles a native Scheme executable, runs with the
existing 20-second/1-GiB measurement bound, and validates all 88 observations.
Raw/bound/invoke use identical result allocation. Preparation includes graph,
cooking, declaration installation, wrapper binding and admission. No end-to-end
speedup is inferred from the microbenchmark. GC-crossing or negative allocation
deltas are unavailable; their signed diagnostic is retained. Source and binary
identities are checked before/after measurement; historical studies remain pinned
to their original sources.
